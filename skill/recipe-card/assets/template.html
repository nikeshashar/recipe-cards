Roast Tomato & Basil Soup

Macros · per serving
320 kcal 9 g protein 28 g carbs 18 g fat
Ingredients
For the soup
  • 1 kg ripe tomatoes, halved
  • 1 red onion, quartered
  • 3 garlic cloves, unpeeled
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 500 ml vegetable stock
  • 1 small bunch fresh basil
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste
To serve (optional)
  • Crusty bread
Method
  1. Heat the oven to 200°C. Spread the tomatoes, onion and garlic on a tray, drizzle with the olive oil and season well.
  2. Roast for 35–40 minutes until soft and caramelised at the edges.
  3. Squeeze the garlic from its skins and tip everything into a blender with the stock and most of the basil.
  4. Blend until smooth, then reheat gently. Adjust the seasoning to taste.*
  5. Serve topped with the remaining basil and a swirl of olive oil.
