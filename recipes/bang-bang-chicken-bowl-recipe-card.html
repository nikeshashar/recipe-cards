Recipe

Creamy Bang Bang Chicken Bowl

Serves 4
Ingredients
For the chicken
  • 1 lb chicken thighs, finely diced
  • 1/2 tsp avocado oil
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • Salt and pepper
  • 1 tbsp garlic powder
  • 1 tbsp onion powder
  • 1 tbsp smoked paprika
  • Juice of half a large lime
  • 1/2 tbsp powdered monkfruit or sweetener
  • Optional: 1 tbsp gluten free flour or cornstarch
For the base
  • Kaizen high protein lowcarb rice (or any rice you like)
For the cucumber cabbage slaw
  • 1/4 head cabbage, thinly shredded
  • 3–4 Persian cucumbers, thinly sliced
  • 1 carrot, thinly shredded
  • 3 scallions, finely sliced
  • 1 jalapeño, thinly sliced (seeds removed if you like)
  • Large handful cilantro, roughly chopped
  • 1 tbsp sesame oil
  • 3 tbsp rice vinegar
  • 2 tbsp tamari or soy sauce
  • Pinch of salt and pepper
  • 1 tbsp toasted sesame seeds
For the creamy bang bang sauce
  • 3 tbsp mayo
  • 2 tbsp sriracha
  • 1.5 tbsp unsweetened ketchup
  • Juice of half a lime
  • 1/4 tsp cayenne pepper
  • 1/2 tsp powdered monkfruit
  • 2 tbsp water, to thin if you wish
To serve
  • Fresh cilantro
  • Sesame seeds
Method
  1. Chicken. Finely dice the chicken thighs into small pieces. Add to a bowl, drizzle with the avocado oil and soy sauce, and season with salt, pepper, garlic powder, onion powder, smoked paprika, the lime juice, the monkfruit, and the optional flour or cornstarch. Mix well, then bake at 400°F for 32 mins, or air-fry at 400°F for 22 mins.
  2. Base. For the base, use Kaizen high protein lowcarb rice, or any rice you like.
  3. Slaw. Thinly shred the cabbage with a mandolin and add to a bowl with the Persian cucumbers, carrot, scallions, jalapeño (remove seeds if you like) and cilantro. Add the sesame oil, rice vinegar, tamari or soy sauce, and a pinch of salt and pepper. Mix really well, then taste and adjust. Stir in the toasted sesame seeds for a nice crunch.
  4. Bang bang sauce. Mix together the mayo, sriracha, unsweetened ketchup, lime juice, cayenne pepper and powdered monkfruit. Mix well, taste, and add 2 tbsp water to thin the sauce if you wish.
  5. Assemble. Divide into 4 servings. For each, plate up 1 serving of rice as the base, then add 4 oz cooked chicken and a cup of slaw. Drizzle with some of the sauce and top with fresh cilantro and sesame seeds.
