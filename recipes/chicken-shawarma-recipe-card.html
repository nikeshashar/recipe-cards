Freezer Meal Prep

Chicken Shawarma

Serves 8 Marinate 3–20 hrs Freeze up to 3–4 months
Macros · per serving
367 kcal 55 g protein 15 g fat 4 g carbs
Ingredients
  • 2.1 kg boneless skinless chicken thighs
  • 200 g 0% Greek yogurt
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 1/4 cup lemon juice
  • 2 tbsp coriander
  • 2 tbsp cumin
  • 4 tsp cardamom
  • 2 tsp cayenne
  • 4 tsp smoked paprika
  • 4 tsp salt
Method
  1. Prep once. Combine the yogurt, olive oil, garlic, lemon juice and all the spices, then coat the chicken thighs thoroughly in the marinade.
  2. Marinate. Leave for 3–20 hours before cooking or freezing.
  3. Freeze raw. Portion into freezer bags or containers and freeze for up to 3–4 months. Thaw before cooking.
  4. Cook fresh. Cook the chicken through until fully done — 74°C / 165°F in the thickest part.*
Serving ideas: rice or pita, sumac salad, and a Greek yogurt dip.
* The original post gives prep, marinating and freezing instructions but no cooking method — step 4 is general guidance (the temperature is standard food safety). Grill, roast or pan-fry as you prefer.
