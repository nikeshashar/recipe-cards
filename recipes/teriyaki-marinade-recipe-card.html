Chicken Marinade · No. V

Teriyaki

For 6 chicken thighs Scaled ×1.5
Marinade
  • 6 chicken thighs
  • 6 Tbsp soy sauce
  • 1.5 Tbsp honey
  • 1.5 Tbsp rice vinegar
  • 1.5 Tbsp sesame oil
  • 1.5 Tbsp brown sugar
  • 1.5 Tbsp sesame seeds
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1.5 inch piece fresh ginger, grated
How to use
  1. Combine all the marinade ingredients and coat the chicken thoroughly.
  2. Marinate before cooking — a few hours, or overnight in the fridge for deeper flavour.
  3. Cook the chicken through to 165°F / 74°C.
Quantities: the original is written for 4 chicken thighs — everything here is scaled up by half (×1.5) to suit 6, so soy sauce goes from 1/4 cup to 6 Tbsp. How to use is general marinade guidance — the original post listed ingredients only.
