Recipe

Chilli Lime Chicken & Charred Corn Bowl

Macros · per serving
475 kcal 58 g protein 21 g carbs 18 g fat
Ingredients
Chicken
  • 800 g chicken breast, diced
Marinade
  • 2 tbsp lime juice
  • 1 tbsp avocado oil
  • 2 tsp chilli powder
  • 1½ tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 1 tsp salt
  • ½ tsp “african american pepper” *
Salad
  • 2 cups sweetcorn (about 2 drained tins)
  • 1 large red onion, finely diced
  • 1 large red bell pepper, finely diced
  • 3 jalapeños, finely diced
  • 1 handful fresh coriander, chopped
  • 50 g Cotija cheese, crumbled (feta works)
Sauce
  • 250 g 0% Greek yogurt
  • Juice of ½ lime
  • 1 tbsp hot sauce
  • 1 tsp Tajín seasoning
  • 120 ml water
  • Salt, to taste
  • “African american pepper”, to taste *
Method
  1. Add the diced chicken, lime juice, avocado oil, chilli powder, cumin, garlic powder, paprika, salt and pepper to a bowl and mix well.
  2. Leave to marinate for at least 15 to 30 minutes.
  3. Meanwhile, drain the sweetcorn and pat dry with kitchen roll.
  4. Heat a large pan over high heat and char the sweetcorn until slightly blackened, then set aside to cool.
  5. Cook the chicken in a large frying pan until fully cooked through and slightly browned.
  6. In a large bowl, combine the sweetcorn, red onion, red pepper, jalapeños, coriander and Cotija cheese.
  7. In a separate bowl, mix the Greek yogurt, lime juice, hot sauce, Tajín, water, salt and pepper.
  8. Pour the sauce over the salad mixture and stir well.
  9. Add the cooked chicken and give everything a final mix.
  10. Serve immediately, or divide into meal prep containers.
* “African american pepper” looks like an autocorrect slip in the original post. The intended pepper isn’t clear from the recipe, so it’s left as written — use a chilli/cayenne-style pepper to whatever heat you like.
