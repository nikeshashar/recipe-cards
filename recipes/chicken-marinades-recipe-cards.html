Chicken Marinade · No. I

Hot Honey Harissa

For 6 chicken thighs
Marinade
  • 6 boneless, skinless chicken thighs
  • 3 Tbsp olive oil
  • 3 Tbsp harissa
  • 1 Tbsp tomato paste
  • 1 Tbsp garlic paste
  • 1 Tbsp ginger paste
  • Juice & zest of 1/2 lemon
  • 2 tsp smoked paprika
  • 2 tsp coriander
  • 2 tsp cumin
  • 2 tsp salt (or more to taste after cooking)
  • 2 Tbsp honey
  • Red chilli flakes, to taste
  • Big pinch parsley & cilantro
How to use
  1. Combine all the marinade ingredients and coat the chicken thoroughly.
  2. Marinate before cooking — a few hours, or overnight in the fridge for deeper flavour.
  3. Cook the chicken through to 165°F / 74°C.
Quantities: the original is written for 2 lb chicken thighs. Six thighs is roughly 1.5–2 lb, so the marinade amounts are left unchanged. How to use is general marinade guidance — the original post listed ingredients only.

Cilantro Lime Chicken

For 6 chicken thighs
Marinade
  • 6 chicken thighs
  • 1/4 cup chopped fresh cilantro leaves
  • 5–10 fresh basil leaves (optional)
  • 2 Tbsp Greek yogurt
  • 1 Tbsp olive oil
  • 2 Tbsp freshly squeezed lime juice
  • 1 Tbsp lime zest
  • 2 tsp chili powder
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp kosher salt
  • 1 tsp freshly ground black pepper
How to use
  1. Blend or mix the marinade ingredients, then coat the chicken thoroughly.
  2. Marinate before cooking — a few hours, or overnight in the fridge for deeper flavour.
  3. Cook the chicken through to 165°F / 74°C.
Quantities: the original is written for 2 lb chicken thighs. Six thighs is roughly 1.5–2 lb, so the marinade amounts are left unchanged. How to use is general marinade guidance — the original post listed ingredients only.

Chicken Marinade · No. III

Sweet & Spicy Korean

For 6 chicken thighs Scaled ×1.5
Marinade
  • 6 chicken thighs, fat trimmed
  • 5–7 cloves garlic, minced
  • 3 heaping Tbsp gochujang paste
  • 3 Tbsp soy sauce
  • 3 Tbsp oyster sauce
  • 1.5 tsp rice vinegar
  • 1–2 dashes fish sauce
  • 1.5 tsp sesame oil
  • 1.5 Tbsp gochugaru (more if you like it spicier)
  • Pinch of salt
  • 1.5 tsp cracked black pepper
  • 4.5 Tbsp water
  • Sliced scallions (optional)
How to use
  1. Combine all the marinade ingredients and coat the chicken thoroughly.
  2. Marinate before cooking — a few hours, or overnight in the fridge for deeper flavour.
  3. Cook the chicken through to 165°F / 74°C; finish with scallions.
Quantities: the original is written for 4 chicken thighs — everything here is scaled up by half (×1.5) to suit 6. Pinch of salt and dash of fish sauce don't scale neatly, so adjust to taste. How to use is general marinade guidance — the original post listed ingredients only.

Chicken Marinade · No. IV

Sorta Somali Suqaar

For 6 chicken thighs
Marinade
  • 6 chicken thighs
  • 1 large sweet onion
  • 1 Tbsp tomato paste
  • 1 Tbsp garlic paste (or 6–7 cloves garlic)
  • 1 Tbsp ginger paste
  • 3 Tbsp oil of choice
  • 1.5 Tbsp Xawaash spice
  • 1 Tbsp paprika
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 1 tsp coriander
  • 1 tsp turmeric (optional)
  • 1 tsp cayenne (more if you like spicy)
  • 1/2 tsp mustard powder (optional, or use regular mustard)
  • Salt, pepper & MSG to taste
How to use
  1. Dice the chicken and onion. Combine with the marinade ingredients and coat well.
  2. Marinate before cooking — a few hours, or overnight in the fridge for deeper flavour.
  3. Cook the chicken through to 165°F / 74°C.
Quantities: the original is written for 1–1.5 lb chicken thighs. Six thighs is roughly 1.5–2 lb, so amounts are left unchanged — bump the spices slightly if your thighs are large. How to use is general guidance; suqaar is usually pan-fried diced meat — the original post listed ingredients only.

  3. Cook the chicken through to 165°F / 74°C.
Quantities: the original is written for 4 chicken thighs — everything here is scaled up by half (×1.5) to suit 6, so soy sauce goes from 1/4 cup to 6 Tbsp. How to use is general marinade guidance — the original post listed ingredients only.
