Spice Blend

Homemade Digestive Mix

For sudden stomach pain, gas, acidity or indigestion
Ingredients
  • 30 g roasted fennel seeds (saunf)
  • 20 g roasted cumin seeds (jeera)
  • 10 g roasted carom seeds (ajwain)
  • 10 g roasted coriander seeds (dhaniya)
  • 10 g dried pomegranate seed powder (anar dana powder)
  • 2 tsp dried mint powder
  • 2 tsp rock salt
  • 2 tsp cardamom powder (elaichi powder)
  • 2 tsp hing (asafoetida)
Method
  1. Grind all the ingredients.
  2. Store in an airtight container. Keeps for up to one month.
How and when to consume — Take one teaspoon with a glass of lukewarm water, whenever you feel gas or heaviness.
Shared by the source as a traditional home remedy — a digestive churna — not medical advice.
